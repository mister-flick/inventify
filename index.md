
# Inventify

### Intelligent IT Asset Management for macOS

Inventify is an independent software project designed to simplify IT equipment inventory, asset lifecycle management, document organization, and reporting.

## Features

- IT asset inventory and tracking
- Equipment search and filtering
- Asset lifecycle and maintenance management
- Document and report generation
- QR codes and equipment labels
- AI assistant integration (in development)

## AI Integration

Inventify is developing an AI assistant to help users interact with inventory data through natural language.

We are exploring official OpenAI authentication and authorized ChatGPT subscription integration.
