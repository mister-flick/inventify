01 The product

IT asset management,
with the context intact.

Equipment records are most useful when the surrounding details are easy to find. Inventify is being shaped around one focused workspace for the information that follows an asset through its life.

Inventify is currently in development. The capabilities on this page describe the project’s intended scope; availability and details may change as the app takes shape.

03 AI roadmap

✳ In development

A useful assistant,
with a clear purpose.

Inventify is developing an assistant that could help people work with inventory data through natural-language questions.

The goal is practical help with the information in an asset workspace—not AI for its own sake. Its capabilities, data flows and supported services remain under development.
